BIRTHSTONE LORE

Every tradition of birthstones on one wheel. 38 lists read from Kunz's 1913 Curious Lore of Precious Stones, shown side by side, with birth profiles, a stone dictionary and widgets. Runs offline. Astronomy from AstroEngine, measured against NASA JPL. Native on iPad in every orientation, with an optional sidebar, and ready for iPhone Duo in every pose.

Birthstones app for iPhone and iPad. 38 traditions of birthstones by month, zodiac sign, weekday, hour, season and planet, from the breastplate of Aaron to the Hindu navaratna and the jewellers' list of 1912, each read from George Frederick Kunz's The Curious Lore of Precious Stones (1913) and shown side by side. Birth profiles gather every stone that applies to one person, with the sign worked out from the true position of the Sun. A dictionary of 93 stones gives historical names, mineralogy from Dana and lore quoted from Kunz. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, an optional daily notification, no account and fully offline. Runs natively on iPad in every orientation, with an optional sidebar in place of the tab bar, and is ready for iPhone Duo, setting the wheel and the comparison grid across the inner display and keeping the stones of the present and the last list clear of the tab bar at the side of the outer one. Its astronomy comes from AstroEngine, the on-device engine shared across our sky apps and measured against NASA JPL Horizons.
