Irish airfield guide for iPhone and iPad. Every airfield on the island of Ireland, North and South, from Dublin, Shannon and Belfast to grass farm strips, microlight fields, island aerodromes, heliports and the seaplane base on Lough Derg. 153 open fields with runways, surfaces and lighting, radio frequencies in the order you would use them, elevation and position, distance and bearing from where you stand, dawn and dusk at the field, and the live METAR and TAF for every field that reports one. A Radio tab gathers every airfield frequency on the island alongside Shannon VOLMET, the HF weather broadcast from County Clare. Heritage tells the stories of 33 lost airfields, from wartime RAF stations and the Lough Erne flying-boat base to forgotten farm strips and estate runways, each written from cited sources you can open and read. A logbook turns every field you visit into a passport stamp, with a gold star for the fields you verify standing at the fence, and poster-style cards to share. Airfield data from OurAirports. Runs natively on iPad in every orientation, the field sliding in beside the map, and is ready for iPhone Duo, opening across the whole of the inner display and carrying an open airfield across a fold. No account, no subscription, and the guide works without a connection. Built for pilots, spotters and airband listeners.
