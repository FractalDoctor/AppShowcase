HF propagation app for iPhone and iPad. Real-time shortwave band conditions from NOAA space weather data. Check which ham radio bands are open — 160m through 10m — before you transmit. Runs natively on iPad, with larger type and the layout opened out for the bigger screen. Its astronomy comes from AstroEngine, the on-device engine shared across our sky apps and measured against NASA JPL Horizons.
